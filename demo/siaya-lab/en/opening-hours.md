# Opening hours

Siaya Community Computer Lab (planned).

The lab is not open yet. The hours below are illustrative values for the
demo; the lab's real hours will be set before it opens.

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Monday to Friday | 09:00 to 18:00 |
| Saturday | 09:00 to 18:00 |
| Sunday | Closed |
| Public holidays | Closed |

- The last booking starts one hour before closing.
- The staff on duty may close the lab early during a power cut. Hub Steward
  posts a message in the lab's room when this happens.
- Hub Steward answers in the chat at any time, also when the lab is closed.
  Access requests and bookings made outside opening hours are confirmed by
  the lab manager on the next opening day.
