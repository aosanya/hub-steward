# Wi-Fi guide

Siaya Community Computer Lab (planned). Illustrative values for the demo.

## Networks

| Network | Who it is for | Password |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| `SiayaLab-Members` | Members, on their own phones and laptops | Your personal Wi-Fi login |
| `SiayaLab-Guest` | Visitors, for one day | A day code from the staff on duty |

The lab computers are connected by cable and need no Wi-Fi.

## Getting your Wi-Fi login

Your Wi-Fi login is part of your lab access. Ask Hub Steward for access in
the lab's chat. When the lab manager approves, your personal login is created
and you collect it at the lab terminal together with your member barcode.

The login is yours alone. Do not share it.

## Connecting

1. Open your device's Wi-Fi settings.
2. Choose `SiayaLab-Members`.
3. Enter the username and password from your Wi-Fi login.
4. If a page opens asking you to accept the house rules, accept them.

## Limits

- Up to two devices per member at the same time.
- Each member has a fair-use allowance of 2 GB a day. After that the
  connection slows down until the next day.
- Large downloads, streaming films and file sharing are not allowed during
  opening hours, so that everyone can work.

## When the Wi-Fi does not work

1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again on your device.
2. Check that you chose `SiayaLab-Members`, not the guest network.
3. Ask Hub Steward. It can see whether the lab's network has a problem and
   tells the staff on duty.

If your login stopped working, ask Hub Steward to check it or ask the staff
for a new one.
