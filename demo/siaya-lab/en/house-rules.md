# House rules

Siaya Community Computer Lab (planned). Illustrative rules for the demo.

The lab is shared by everyone who comes. These rules keep it open, safe and
useful for all members.

## Who may use the lab

- Any registered member may use the lab during opening hours.
- To become a member, ask Hub Steward in the lab's chat for access. The lab
  manager approves the request, and you finish set-up at the lab terminal,
  where you receive your member barcode.
- Bring your barcode on your phone or printed. Show it at the desk when you
  arrive.

## In the lab

1. Sign in at the desk when you arrive and sign out when you leave.
2. Use only the seat you booked. If your seat is taken, ask the staff on duty.
3. No food at the seats. Water in a closed bottle is fine.
4. Keep your voice low. Use headphones for sound and video calls.
5. Do not unplug, open or move equipment. Report any fault to the staff on
   duty or to Hub Steward.
6. Do not install software on the lab computers. Ask the staff if you need a
   program.
7. Save your work to your own account or a USB stick. The lab computers are
   cleared at the end of each day.
8. Children under 14 come with an adult member.

## Respect

- Treat every member and the staff with respect. Harassment, threats and
  discrimination are not allowed.
- Do not look at or share content that is illegal, violent or sexual.
- Do not use another member's account, barcode or Wi-Fi login.

## If a rule is broken

The staff on duty may ask a member to leave for the day. The lab manager may
suspend a membership after a serious or repeated breach, and will tell the
member why.

## Questions

Ask Hub Steward in the lab's room in the member app, or the staff on duty.
