# Booking rules

Siaya Community Computer Lab (planned). Illustrative rules for the demo.

The lab has 20 seats. A booking keeps a seat for you.

## How to book

- Ask Hub Steward in the lab's chat, for example: "Can I book a seat
  tomorrow from 10 to 12?"
- Hub Steward drafts the booking and the lab manager confirms it. You get a
  message when it is confirmed.
- You can also book at the desk with the staff on duty.

## Limits

1. A booking is for one or two hours.
2. Each member may book up to six hours a week.
3. You may book up to seven days ahead.
4. One seat per member at a time.

## Arriving

- Arrive on time. If you are more than 15 minutes late and have not told the
  lab, the seat is given to someone else.
- Show your member barcode at the desk.

## Cancelling

- Cancel as soon as you know you cannot come, by telling Hub Steward or the
  staff.
- A member who misses three bookings in a month without cancelling may not
  book for the following week.

## Walk-ins

Seats that are not booked are free for members who walk in, for one hour at a
time. Booked members go first.

## Courses and groups

The lab manager may reserve several seats for a course or a member group.
Those seats show as taken when you book.
