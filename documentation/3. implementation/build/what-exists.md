## Harvest sale

The console and the app carry a harvest sale in two jobs. The Suppliers' agent asks the one buyer a farmer picked, once, and holds no farmer threads. The Farmers' agent tells the farmer once that the buyer said yes or no. Neither answers for anyone. The buyer's yes is the market's own record, kept with its date and note. Silence changes nothing. The older produce matcher is gone: a farmer sees buyers' posted prices and picks one.

## Places

The spec language has a `geometry` field type holding a GeoJSON Point, Polygon or MultiPolygon, checked on write. A group carries an optional boundary (Polygon or MultiPolygon), set on creation or with `PUT /groups/{group_id}/boundary`. The geo package finds the ward a point falls in, and `GET /groups/at-point?lon=&lat=` lists the groups whose boundary holds a point (a ward index built from the boundaries). A raised item with a pin and no area, and a catalog entry whose doc has a `location` point and no `ward`, get the innermost group boundary holding the point (the host wires it through the catalog's area resolver); a typed area or ward is kept and a disagreement is flagged in `area_mismatch` or `ward_mismatch`. Not built: drawing boundaries in the console.

A raised item carries a `department` and a `department_by` (`rule` or `officer`). The catalog `routing` setting (`{"default": ..., "rules": [{"topic": ..., "department": ...}]}`) fills the department from the topic when the item is filed; a department an officer typed when filing on someone's behalf, or set with `PUT /raised-items/{raisedItemID}/department` (`catalog.raised_item.reassign`, a tool like its siblings, granted by `board.manage`), is kept. A resident's `GET /raised-items/mine` and the follow-key read show the department and a status (`received` until grouped under a board item, then `open`, the board's outcome, or `closed`). Not built: re-running the rules on existing items when the setting changes.

## Approval limits

A seat can carry an `approval_limit`, set when it is granted or changed on a live seat with `SetApprovalLimit` (a negative one, or an ended seat, is refused). `AuthoriseApproval` loads the seat and refuses an amount over its limit with `ErrOverApprovalLimit` (403). Not built: the forms approval flow does not call it yet, because a form carries no amount; no HTTP route or MCP tool for the setter or the check.

A form carries an `amount` (zero means it commits nothing; a negative one is refused). When it is above zero, `Approve` and `ApproveSlot` refuse with `ErrOverApprovalLimit` (403) unless the approver holds a live seat whose limit covers it, and refuse the same way when the host wired no seat source. The agency registry wires the check from the approver's live seats. `SetApprovalLimit` and `AuthoriseApproval` are also routes (`PUT /role-assignments/{id}/approval-limit`, `POST /role-assignments/{id}/authorise-approval`) and MCP tools (`actor_set_approval_limit`, `actor_authorise_approval`); setting a limit sits under the `role.assign` permission.

## Answers that land in a record

A form can declare `lands_in` (`record` written `<module>.<record>`, `keyed_by`, `field_map` from question to field), editable while the form is a draft. `Publish` is refused with `ErrUnmappedQuestions` (409), naming the questions, while any current question has no field in `field_map`, and with `ErrNoRecordWriter` (409, "no writer for module X") while no writer is wired for the record's module, so residents never meet the gap. Each send is composed with `LandsIn.Land` (key context: `form`, `member`, `send`, `period`, plus the mapped fields) and written through the `RecordWriter` that `UseRecordWriters` returns for the module; a failed write answers `ErrLandingFailed` (502) after the answers are kept. The catalog, accounting and ratings have writers. The catalog writer puts a catalog record on the `forms` mount (`PutRecord`: subject is the record name, holder is the key as JSON, so a resend on the same key updates the one record). The accounting writer posts one ledger entry for `accounting.entry` (a form fills the two accounts, the whole amount, dates, narration and dimensions; the actor is the member in the key, the document is the form, and a resend on the same key posts nothing a second time). The ratings writer puts `ratings.rating` as the member in the key (a form fills only the item and the value, 0 to 1, and a resend updates). A writer that implements `FieldChecker` has the mapped field names checked at `Publish` too, so a typo (`ammount`) is refused as `ErrInvalidForm` naming the field before residents answer; the accounting and ratings writers do. An edit (`PATCH /forms/{id}`) whose body carries no `lands_in` keeps the one the form has; a body that carries one replaces it. A test run (`ForRun`) composes the record and publishes under the same refusals but writes nothing. Not built: writers for any other module; a resend after `ErrLandingFailed` is the only retry.

A form can declare `one_voice` when it is composed (never changed after; refused on a public or standing form, which have no verified contact to hold a person to): `refuse` or `flag`. The first send by a member in a period is their voice; a second send by the same member in that period, as the same group or another, answers `ErrSecondVoice` (409) under `refuse` with the first answer standing, and under `flag` is stored beside it with `repeat_of` set to the voice's send and left out of `ListAllAnswers`, a member's own answers, the register's respondent count and `GetSend` of the voice. Answers sit in a slot of (question, member, group, period, `repeat_of`, `withdrawn_at`), so an answer a withdrawal hid is never overwritten or deleted by a re-answer: the new answer is stored beside it, still hidden and stored. The old `question_group` and `form_member_group` unique indexes are dropped on provision and replaced by `question_slot` and `form_member_slot`. Not built: the member id is the verified contact, so two members who share a contact are not matched here; two sends at the same instant can both become a voice; restoring withdrawn answers does not yet skip a slot that holds a newer answer.
