# Menu screen pattern

How a screen in the menu is built once it has been restyled. The reference is the resident page (`code/app/src/app/menu/sections/residents/PersonScreen.tsx`) and its mockup, `residents/resident.html` in the municipality repo's `documentation/2. design/mockups/app/`. Every screen that shows a chosen record follows it; a screen with no record (a map, a calendar, a plain report) keeps its own body but takes the bottom bar and the chat.

## The page

- **Title:** the chosen record's name is the page title, with its status as one quiet line under it. There is no header band and no section caption above the title.
- **Cards:** each section is a square card from the shared `Section` (`shared/ui/Section.tsx`): a header with the large section word, the count and a chevron that collapses it; the rows divided by hairlines; a footer of full-width items divided by hairlines. A card shows at most three rows.
- **Footer:** at most two items, in a fixed order: See all with the count on the left (neutral text colour), the act on the right (accent colour, bold). With three rows or fewer there is no See all and the act is the whole footer. An act names its object ("Add to a group", "Start an application", "Raise on their behalf", "Add holding"); it shows only to a reader who holds its permission, and a footer that would be empty is not drawn. An empty card carries the same act in its footer.
- **Rows:** a list-type row is a media object (`ListRow` inside `Section`): a square icon tile, the title in a medium weight, a lighter line, a light square status tag (`statusTone` in `Chip.tsx`: Open and received in the warning hue, paid and done in the success hue, anything else neutral) and a chevron. Facts, such as the Personal detail card, are plain label and value rows with no tile (`plain`).
- **One Edit screen:** a record is edited on one screen opened by Edit in the first card's footer, not by several buttons on the page. A register row (business, informal trader) is edited by a replacing row, so its Edit shows only to someone who may add rows to the register (`can_add_rows`), and the Owner or Holder row opens the resident.
- **See all:** a section's own screen (`/:section`) carries the filter strip (`shared/ui/SectionFilter.tsx`): status tabs with counts when two or more statuses exist, a search row past three rows, a count line, and "Show all" when nothing matches.

## The shell

- The screen is a `MenuWindow` with `withTabs` and `bandless`: the usual bottom bar (Home, Chat, Notifications) sits under it, and the list stays beside the page on a wide screen.
- On a phone a record opened from a link has a Back arrow named after the list (when there is no origin), or an "All <list>" row above the page (when there is one), so the list is always reachable.
- Every screen under a menu section has a Back arrow, wide or phone: it goes to where the person came from, and when the page was opened by its address, after a reload or from a link with no origin it goes to the section the address names (`backOriginOf` in `useBackToSection.ts`). Only the section page itself and the bottom-bar roots have none; `useBackToSection.test.tsx` walks every section path and fails when a new page has no way back.
- **Chat:** the Chat tab opens the assistant about what is on screen, with an "About <name>" line that can be cleared. The carried subject (`app/chatAbout.ts`) names its kind: resident, group, registration, conversation, business, trader, and later the kinds of the other sections. The assistant reads the subject only through existing read tools and the asking user's own permissions, offers pressable choices for that kind, and answers a general question with an overview.

## The Add act on a list

A list that can create what it lists carries one full-width primary button named after its object ("Add department", "Add holding", "Add work order") above Search, in `MenuWindow`'s `above` slot. It is the shared `AddButton` (`app/menu/AddButton.tsx`, the permission check over `AddAct`), and it shows only to a reader who holds the permission the create act needs (`member.add`, `group.manage`, `council.manage`, `task.edit`, `service.edit`, `digitaltwin.manage`, `asset.manage`); the registers of Businesses and Informal traders use `RecordAddButton`, which asks the hub whether the register may add rows, the same gate as their Edit. The button never sits in the title bar or beside the title, and an empty list keeps the act in its empty card instead of showing it twice. It opens the one edit screen of the object in Add mode, never a second screen beside its Edit: the same route family (`/new` and `/:id/edit`), the same title pattern ("Add work order", "Edit work order"), the same `MenuEditForm`, Save and Cancel, and a refusal said in words on the form; a phone or email another person already holds reads "Phone already in use by another person." with the field's own label (`duplicateWords` in `shared/wakala`, used by `refusalWords`, never the hub's raw text), and the typed value stays in the box. An object whose edit was a form inside its detail card gets a routed edit screen on the shared `FormScreen` (`app/menu/FormScreen.tsx`: a load for the edit, the create and update tools, the fields and the two builders); its Edit act in the card footer navigates there. Departments, Crews, Maintenance Schedule and Boundaries take Add mode on their existing Edit screen, the council lists on `CouncilEditScreen`, and the asset lists (Routes, Equipment, Facilities, Buildings) add an asset of the list's category with a name and a tag, then edit it on the same screen. An area is added with a name only because its corners and node need a group that exists. Lists with no Add on purpose: the read lists (Budget by neighbourhood, Payments, Reports), Applications (it lists what the signed-in person sent; Start an application needs the person it is for, so it is an act on the person's page, and it lists only the forms sent to a group the person is in, read with `loadFormsReaching`; if the hub still refuses, the sheet says "<Name> is not in the group this application is for."), Roles & Permissions (a role is defined in the agency's design and given to a person from the person's page, not created from the list) and Agendas & Minutes (no create tool by design).

## Type scale

A card page uses three sizes and no others, all in rem against the 90% root: the page title at 1.5rem, and everything that carries content at 1rem (card heading, row title, the value under a label, the lighter line of a media-object row in list panes and detail panes alike, the quiet line under the title); counts, tags, the right-hand text of a row and tab counts sit one step below at .875rem. The value under a label is told apart from its label by weight and the soft ink, not by being smaller: the soft ink holds a contrast of 9.29 to 1 on the light surface and 7.28 to 1 on the dark one, so only the size was the problem. A multi-line value takes a line height of 1.4. The scale is set once in `section.css` and the row rules of `app.css`; a screen never sets its own font size. The rest of the shared styles follow the same three sizes: the header title is 1rem bold, the agency line under it, the bottom-bar tab labels, form labels and errors, section headings, pane header labels, the unread count and the small lines of chat, backlogs and files are .875rem, and the content variable `--content` is 1rem. Only glyph sizes (chevrons and the clear icon), relative code (.9em) and the headings of a rendered document keep their own size.

## A field that refers to something is picked, not typed

A form field that points at a person, a property, an asset or a group is a searchable picker that reads the agency's own list and shows names, never a typed box and never an id or a code. The shared loaders are in `app/menu/referenceChoices.ts` (`loadHolderChoices` for people and the groups that hold places, `loadPersonIdChoices` for a field stored as an id) and `properties/records.ts` (`loadPropertyChoices`). A person with no name reads "Unnamed person". The picker is `shared/ui/ReferencePicker.tsx`: a list titled by the field with Back, Search and media-object rows, the chosen row marked; full-screen on the phone and a side list from 56rem wide (`SearchSelect` stays for short fixed lists). Add holding is the worked case: the Holder comes from the registry and the groups, and sends the person's actor id with the name, and the Property from the Properties list, with the digital twin's nodes only as the fallback when no property has a place (MUN1514, MUN1523).

## Where it was settled

The resident page and its cards are ISS-20 to ISS-25 and MUN1452 to MUN1465; the Households, Groups, Registrations, Communications, Businesses and Informal traders screens are ISS-28 to ISS-33; the chat subject is ISS-34. The other menu sections follow in ISS-35 (Properties & Addresses), ISS-36 (Issues & Requests), ISS-37 (Services & Permits), ISS-38 (Assets & Works), ISS-39 (Council & Decisions), ISS-40 (Staff & Departments), ISS-41 (Finance) and ISS-42 (Settings); each starts by walking its screens and recording which of them show a record.

A standalone screen that sits under a list or a section (the risk gaps under the risk list, the council risks under Decisions) gets its Back from the same `useBackToSection` hook, through its entry in `standaloneParents`, and never through an `onBack` written on the page (MUN1521). The risk list and the backlogs gate sit under Home through the same table (MUN1524), and `useBackToSection.test.tsx` reads every source that draws a `Shell` and fails when it neither wires a back control nor is one of the three bottom-bar roots (`TabPage`, `HomePage`, `ChatPage`), so a new screen cannot lose its Back.

The wording of the card guide for screens built in code is in [phone-app-structure.md](phone-app-structure.md).
