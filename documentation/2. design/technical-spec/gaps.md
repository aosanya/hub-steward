## The hackathon's focus, most important first

1. **MCP tools for chat, groups, invitations, join requests and forms**, generated by the shared engine behind the membership check.
2. **An agent identity**: grant the `agent` role, a key scoped to one agency, and an answer to member app decision 34.
3. **"Messages since" and a feed**, so the agent reacts to new messages without re-reading threads.
4. **Documents**: mount sources, upload and extract text, embed on the server, answer with citations.
5. **Draft and approve**: one record for "agent drafts, manager approves", and a one-tap approve screen.
6. **Seat booking** with time slots on assetmanager holds.
7. **Network watch**: mount the digital twin, add a collector for router data, give Wi-Fi accounts a home.
8. **A lab agency template**: Lab manager, IT manager, member and Hub Steward roles, RACI, rules.
9. **Barcode set-up**: QR for member and group codes, and sign-in for people without email.
10. **The demo deploy**: member app (MF11), approval of the demo agency (WK126), env (WK61).
11. **Member memory** (stretch).
12. **Issues and votes** (stretch): a standing issue form whose sends land as cases, and votes on cases.
