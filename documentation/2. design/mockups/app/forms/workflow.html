What a member sees of a task. A task is not one screen: a member sees only the steps of her own role, each as a row on Start, and Send moves the run to the next role. A step with a screen of its own opens that screen, any other form step opens the step page, a sent step stays as a Sent row that says Done or whose turn is next, and a chat task opens in chat. Ongoing has two tabs, Live and Done: Live lists the orders and requests that have screens of their own, the ones waiting on her first, and Done keeps what is finished. Drawn from the app's code; the bottom bar is the shared one of tabs.html, with no tab chosen, and the fields of the step page are on form.html.
