What a member sees of a workflow. A workflow is not one screen: a member sees only the steps of her own role, each as a row on Start, and Send moves the run to the next role. A step with a screen of its own opens that screen, any other form step opens the step page, a sent step stays as a Sent row, and a chat workflow opens in chat. Ongoing has two tabs, Live and Done: Live lists the orders and requests that have screens of their own, the ones waiting on her first, and Done keeps what is finished (not built yet). Drawn from the app's code; the bottom bar is the shared one of tabs.html, with no tab chosen, and the fields of the step page are on form.html.
