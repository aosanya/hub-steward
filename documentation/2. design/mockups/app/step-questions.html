Why not just a simple form with the step's questions? The step page of the mayor's step WI-43-3 shows the step title and one placeholder question, because 252 of the 264 form steps in the Hungarian municipality template carry only that placeholder, while the template already holds the real question for this step. Drawn for the owner to discuss; nothing here is built. From section 2 on, each step page is split into three labelled blocks, What to do, Done before you and Your answer, and the mayor's step is reworded in the second person (Give your reason); screen 1 keeps the template's own words. The fields of the step page, every kind of question and the refusals are on form.html, and the Start row on workflow.html. From section 2 on, the questions are the ones the workflow review proposed for W-43, with short plain labels in the style of the Siaya farmers template (Crop, What you see, Is it spreading). Section 7 draws all five steps of W-43 in order, each filled in with invented example values.
