The journey

Send a report

A member sends a report, an idea or a complaint through a form type that opens as an app, and gets a reference.

1. Picks what it is about

The form type opens as a full-screen chat. The agent asks what it is about and offers the themes as prompts she can tap or write herself.

Open the mockup

2. Fills in the form

The other questions of the form come as one block in the chat, filled by the form's questions. Send is her own button.

Open the mockup

3. Gets a reference

The send lands as a report. The chat shows its reference and the two dates the council owes: when it will acknowledge it and when it will answer.

Open the mockup

Result: Her report is filed with a reference and the two dates the council owes.
