Hub Steward · 10:05

Lab-scoped agent key

weak

It reaches its own lab only, but every member can read every document in the lab.

It stands on 2 threats:

  • An answer quotes a document the asker may not read
  • The agent acts beyond its role

If the key fails, both threats are open at once.
