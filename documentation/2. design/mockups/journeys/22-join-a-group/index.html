Join a group

A member asks to join a group, or accepts an invitation to one, and finds its room.

1. Asks to join a group

Join a group is a row on Start, which she opens by tapping Start on Home. It opens a screen that takes a group code and a Find button, or she asks to join one of the groups the agency lists.

Open the mockup

2. Waits for the Admin

After she asks, the group's row reads Requested until the Admin, Grace Adhiambo, decides, with no countdown. The request is also a row under Ongoing, which she opens by tapping Ongoing on Home, the group's name over Join a group and Requested, and it opens the page where she asked.

Open the mockup

3. Is let in

After the Admin approves, the group's row reads You are in. The group's room is on Chat, and its row opens the room.

Open the mockup

4. Is invited from Home

On Home the strip counts the invitation and opens the Notifications page, where it is a row under Needs you. Opened, it names the group and who invited her, how long it has left, and has Decline and Join. After Join the card says she joined and where the room is, and has no buttons.

Open the mockup

Result: She is in the group and its room is on Chat.
