Sell the harvest

Sells the harvest to a buyer, alone or together with other farmers.

1. Sees the buyers' prices and sells now

Asha Otieno opens the Sell harvest row on the Start tab and the Farmers' agent asks what she wants to sell. She says maize and gets the best buyer first: what each buyer pays for a 90 kg bag, for example Kisumu Grain Traders at KES 4,200 a bag and KES 4,350 from 100 bags, and the group sale that is open. She chooses Sell now, enters 40 bags and sells at the price shown. The agent asks the buyer once; the yes is the buyer's own, and until it comes the ask simply has no answer yet. After the yes her sale shows 40 bags, the price it was made at, KES 168,000 in all and the day to collect from her farm, Friday, 6 Nov, with a trail of who asked and who said yes and a Cancel the sale button.

2. Sells together with other farmers

From the buyers' prices Asha opens the open group sale: Kisumu Grain Traders at KES 4,350 a bag from 100 bags, 62 bags so far from 6 farmers, started by the group coordinator. She joins with 40 bags under the terms it started with, and then sees her own line and the new total of 102 bags and 7 farmers, and no other farmer's name or amount. When the sale is sent and the buyer says yes it shows as confirmed: her share of 40 bags at KES 4,350 a bag, KES 174,000 in all, to collect from her farm on Friday, 6 Nov.

3. The buyer posts a price and answers an ask

Kisumu Grain Traders opens Your prices and sees the asks waiting, for example Asha Otieno with 40 bags of maize ready, beside the prices it has posted. Post a price takes the product in the buyer's own words, the unit, the price and the bulk price from a number of bags. An ask shows the farmer's name and bags and no contact details, the buyer's price and the total, KES 168,000. The buyer answers with their own button, Yes or No; Yes asks for a date to collect and a note. After Yes the ask shows 40 bags from Asha Otieno, the date, the note and KES 168,000 at KES 4,200 a bag.
