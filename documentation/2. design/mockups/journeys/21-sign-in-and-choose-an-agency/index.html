Sign in and choose an agency

A member signs in with an emailed link and goes into one of her agencies.

1. Signs in with an emailed link

Sign in is an email field and one button that sends a link, then Change language. After sending, the form stays on screen so a mistyped address can be corrected; the screen says only that a link is on its way if the address can sign in.

Open the mockup

2. Picks a language

Change language opens Select Language: the nine languages, each written in itself, English shaded, and a Back link to Sign in. Choosing one returns to Sign in in that language.

Open the mockup

3. Sees her agencies

Your agencies has one card for each agency she belongs to, with its id under its name and, where forms are waiting, their count. Find an agency sits under the list after a divider. With none, one card says the agencies she joins will appear here. A member in one agency never sees this screen.

Open the mockup

4. Finds an agency and joins it

Find an agency is one field and a Search button; with nothing typed, the listed agencies by name, each with its kind and its id. An agency page says what it is for, how the agency is joined, and has one button that reads Join or Ask to join as the agency chose.

Open the mockup

5. Opens an agency's Home

Home of the Siaya Farmers' Group opens with a strip, You have N things to do, that opens the Needs you section of Notifications, then lists Start and Ongoing as option cards (Backlogs is a third card only when the task module is on, and Home shows only the cards the agency and role have). Tapping Start opens the agency's tasks as plain rows: Buy inputs, Sell harvest and Forms, then Join a group and Apply as a supplier. The Community Computer Lab's Start has Join a group as its only row. The bar holds the page title and the hamburger button at the right; a screen opened from a card names the agency under its title. The hamburger opens a drawer from the right, the page sliding left, with My profile, Switch agency, Change language and Sign out, and Switch agency opens Your agencies. The same button is on Chat and Notifications. The bottom bar reads Home, Chat and Notifications in every agency, and a screen opened from an option keeps it with none chosen (decisions 154 and 155). The list keeps a task after she begins it; what she has begun and not finished is a row under Ongoing, and with nothing begun Ongoing is one card, Nothing ongoing yet, with Go to Start inside it.
