The journey

Review an agency

The designer presses one button and AI reviewers read the whole draft at once, one for each part of it, and she reads their findings one tab per part.

1. Opens Review

Review is a child row under Publish. With no review yet, one card says so and carries Run review. Otherwise the latest review shows.

Open the mockup

2. Runs the review

One progress card says "Reading 3 of 6 parts" until every reviewer has finished; the tabs appear then. She may leave the page and come back to the same run.

Open the mockup

3. Reads a part

One tab each for Goals, Objectives (farmers' agency only), Roles and agents, Workflows, Risks and Tests. A tab opens with a two-sentence summary and one row per finding.

Open the mockup

4. Answers a finding

Apply changes the draft and marks the finding Applied; Dismiss marks it Dismissed; Details opens a tab; Ask the chat about this opens the chat. The state is saved in the database.

Open the mockup

5. Comments on a finding

A comment box on the finding row saves her note in the database. The next review reads it.

Open the mockup

6. Runs the review again

The new review reads the previous one and her comments, then scans the whole draft again. Each earlier finding shows its fate: new, fixed, still open or withdrawn. A dismissed finding stays dismissed unless her comment no longer explains it.

Open the mockup

7. Finds a part that failed

The part's tab says Failed and gives the reason. The other tabs keep their findings.

Open the mockup

Result: The draft has been read by every reviewer, the findings are saved in the database, and she has applied, dismissed or commented on each one. A later review reads her comments and says what became of every earlier finding.
