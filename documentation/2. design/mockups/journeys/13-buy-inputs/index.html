Buy inputs

Buys farm inputs from the shops she picks, alone or together with other farmers.

1. Opens the market

She opens Buy inputs, a row on the Start tab of her group. The Farmers' agent asks what she wants to buy and lists nothing up front, because the products may be many. She answers "Urea" and sees the cheapest shops, one card each, in the shop's own words with its price and its own Buy now button: Kisumu Agro Supplies, Urea 50kg, KES 3,800 a bag; Siaya Farm Stores, UREA 46% 50KG, KES 3,950 a bag; and Show 1 more shop brings Bondo Agrovet, urea (bag), KES 4,100 a bag. A bulk price sits under a shop's price: KES 3,750 from 30 bags at Siaya Farm Stores, and at Kisumu Agro Supplies an open group order at KES 3,600 from 20 bags with Buy together beside it. She sees every price before she chooses, and nobody is asked anything, because a posted price answers by itself.

Open the mockup

2. Buys now

She presses Buy now beside Kisumu Agro Supplies' Urea 50kg and fills in how many (2 bags) and the day she needs them (Friday, 6 Nov). Her order shows Waiting with the price she saw, KES 3,800 a bag and KES 7,600 in all, and the agent says it is asking the shop. There is no timer: a shop that has not answered simply has no answer yet. When the shop says yes the order shows Yes with the shop's date, at the price she saw. Cancel is her own button on an order that is waiting or has a yes; after it the order shows Cancelled and the agent says it is telling the shop.

3. Joins a group order

From Join beside the open group order she sees the Urea order, started by Grace Adhiambo: the terms it started with, the bags ordered so far and the number of farmers. She sees the total and the count, never another farmer's name or amount. Join the group order asks one thing, how many (2 bags), and Join is her own button. If the shop changes its bulk price, the new terms come to her as a question, "Do you accept KES 3,700 a bag from 20 bags?", beside the terms it started with and her own share (2 bags, KES 7,400). Yes and No are her own buttons, and if she says nothing her line waits, with no timer, until the group coordinator decides. Once the order is sent and the shop says yes, her order shows Confirmed with her share, the confirmed price, the total, the shop's date and its note, "Collect at the shop".

4. Finds something not listed

When what she wants is not on the market she opens Ask for something not listed and fills in what she wants (Hybrid maize seed 614), how much (10 kg) and when (Friday, 6 Nov). The agent says no shop has listed it, and she chooses Ask shops or Put up a notice. Ask shops lists the shops and she ticks the ones to ask; only those are asked, once each. Her page then shows who answered: one shop said yes with a price for 10 kg and a Buy now, another said no, and a third still shows Waiting. A notice shows what she wants, how much and by when to every shop and stays up until she presses Take it down.

5. Sees her orders

My orders lists her Buy now orders and group orders, each with its quantity, how she ordered, the price it was made at and its state, for example Paid for the group order and Cancelled for the Buy now. Opening one shows the order as it was made: the supplier, her share, the price, the total and the date, with a trail of plain dated lines (she joined, the shop asked to revise, she said yes, the shop said yes for Friday, the coordinator marked her paid). She sees only her own line of a group order.
