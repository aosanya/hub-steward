The journey

See where the tokens go

The hub manager sees how many tokens the hub's models used, for what kind of work (chat, workflow run, agency review, test run, background job) and by which person or agent, and finds out why a month's tokens are going fast.

1. Opens Usage and sees the month

The manager of the Farmers' hub opens Usage under Settings, which opens into three pages in the menu: Overview, Kind of work, and Agents and people. Overview is the month so far: four figures (tokens, requests, input against output, the top model) and the tokens of each day stacked by model. Kind of work shows the share of the month for each kind of work (chat, workflow run, agency review, test run, background job) and a grid of models against kinds of work, the darkest square the biggest spender. Above the charts on every page the manager picks Today, 7 days, This month or Last month. The frames are Overview, Kind of work, and the first visit, before any call has been made, where Overview says there are no calls yet. Only managers see these pages; staff seats do not.

2. Sees who spent it, by person and by agent

On Agents and people the Group by switch changes who the tokens are charged to. Person charges them to the member or manager who started the chat or run, with the jobs an agent started on its own under The agent itself, and the bars and the Breakdown table list people; Agent charges them to the agents, and the bars and the table list agents. The first frame is the month grouped by person; the second is the same month grouped by agent.

Open the mockup

3. Narrows everything to one agent by clicking it

The manager clicks an agent's bar on Agents and people and the pages filter themselves to that agent: a chip, Role: Farmers' agent, appears beside the time choice, the bars become Who used Farmers' agent, naming the people, and the Breakdown table lists only that agent. Kind of work redraws for that agent alone too. The x on the chip brings the whole hub back. The frames show Agents and people and Kind of work filtered to the Farmers' agent.

Open the mockup

4. Asks the chat why a kind of work used so much

The manager asks the chat "Why did test runs use so much of the month?" and the answer fills the content area of Overview in a tab named after the question, next to the Overview tab: the tokens the test runs used and their share of the month, the number of runs, the busiest day, the test runs by agent, and the three busiest days with who ran them. The chat says the Hub manager ran the scenarios of both agents again and again. The frame shows the answer tab.
