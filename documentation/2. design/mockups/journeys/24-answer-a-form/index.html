The journey

Answer a form

A member answers a form her group sent, one question at a time, and sees where it went.

1. Sees the forms waiting

Forms, Waiting lists the open forms from every group she is in, closing soonest first, under Closes this week and Later. Each row shows its sender, its question count and its closing date; a form with answers kept on the phone adds Draft.

Open the mockup

2. Answers one question at a time

Every answer is kept on the phone as it is given. Next opens the next question; on the last question, Next opens Review.

Open the mockup

3. Reviews and sends

Review lists every question with its answer and an Edit; nothing is sent before Submit.

Open the mockup

4. Sees the thread it opens

Not built: the app does not open a thread after a send. It shows Form sent, or, for a standing form, a card headed Your thread with the answers. The screen below draws the proposed thread.

Open the mockup

5. Finds what she answered

Forms, Answered lists the open forms she has answered, newest first, each with the date of the last send. A quiet button at the foot opens every form she answered.

Open the mockup

6. Has nothing waiting

When nothing is waiting, one card says so and carries its act: See what you answered switches to Answered.

Open the mockup

Result: The answers are sent once and the form moves to Answered.
