Design index

Every page of the console, one card each. Open a file to see its screens. The side bar of every file links to the other files, so the whole set can be clicked through. Every page has a header strip across the top (brand, hub switcher, breadcrumb, language) and three columns under it: the menu on the left, the content in the middle and the chat on the right, which collapses to a rail. What the chat produces as content is drawn in the content area; approvals and actions stay in the chat.

Three of the eight agencies, one console

The console shows one agency at a time, and its menu follows that agency's template. The Farmers' group is the hub the Model key walk uses. The Lab and the Riders are two of the eight demo templates (demo), drawn as the manager's other example hubs in the hub switcher.
