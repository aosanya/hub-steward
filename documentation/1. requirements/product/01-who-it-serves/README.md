# 01 · Who it serves

Hub Steward serves member organizations. Each one is an agency: a set of
people with roles, groups, rules and workflows, and AI roles that help them.
Nobody uses Hub Steward in production today; every agency in the demo is
synthetic.

## The people and roles

| Who | What they need | What Hub Steward does for them |
|---|---|---|
| **Agency manager** | To run the organization without doing every routine task; to know what the AI roles did | Creates the agency from a template, shows what is waiting for approval, who is in which group, and what each agent has done and spent |
| **Agency designer** | To change how the agency works without breaking it | Edits roles, groups, workflows, forms and rules; reviews the draft; tests it with scenarios; publishes the approved change |
| **Group coordinator** | To let people in and keep a group running | Takes join requests and invitations, sends forms to the group, and runs the group's work, such as a group order |
| **Member** | To reach the organization in their own language and get things done | Chat rooms with the agency's agents, a Notifications page, forms to answer and groups to join; reports to send are planned |
| **Risk owner** | To know what could go wrong with the AI roles and which safeguards are proven | Keeps the hazards, threats and controls, and shows which safeguards hold |
| **Supplier and buyer** | To trade with the group on clear terms | Post prices and answer asks; take part in the group's orders and sales where the agency has a market |
| **AI roles** | A place in the agency with clear limits | Each joins as an agent and acts only through declared tools its role permits; [What it does](../02-what-it-does/README.md#the-approval-rule) says when it waits for a person |

Hub Steward is the product. The Hub Steward role is its front-line AI role: it
answers members and drafts what they ask for, and it is declared by the Siaya
lab and the boda-boda riders. Dispatcher and Assets Keeper
are the riders' other roles; the Farmers' agent and the Suppliers' agent are
the farmers'. The other five demo agencies declare no AI roles.
[User journeys](../../user-journeys/README.md) lists them.

A manager's own title differs by agency: the lab says Lab manager, the riders
Hub manager, the farmers Group coordinator. This page says agency manager for
all of them.

## Example: the computer lab

The first lab is planned for Siaya, Kenya; a lab at a refugee accommodation
in Germany is the next site. Neither is open yet. The Siaya lab is one agency
in the demo, and its two main roles are an example of the table above.

| Who | What they need from the lab | What Hub Steward does for them |
|---|---|---|
| **Lab member** | A seat, Wi-Fi, help, the rules, in their own language | Answers their questions from the lab's documents; takes their request for a seat, a Wi-Fi account or set-up |
| **Lab manager** | Time for people rather than routine | Drafts access requests for the manager to approve |
| **Hub Steward role** | A place in the lab with clear limits | Joins the lab as its agent and acts only through Mwanachama's tools; a guarded change waits for the manager |

- In Siaya, a rural county in western Kenya, members are expected to write
  in Swahili or English.
- In Germany, members are residents of the accommodation who need a computer
  for language courses, school work and job applications; many are new to
  German, and some may have no email address.

The lab's four documents exist in English, Swahili and German, so one set
serves both sites. The Hub Steward role answers in the language a member
writes in because its instructions say so. Signing in without email is an open
question for any agency whose members may have no email address, the German
lab being the example (see [What it does](../02-what-it-does/README.md)).

The members of the other demo agencies are described in each agency's own
README ([demo](../../../../demo/README.md)).

## Other agencies

The demo also holds a farmers' group, boda-boda riders, a clinical research
organization, an electric grid utility, a logistics company, a mobile money
operator and a drone crop-spraying service. A municipality in Hungary is in
conversation about using Hub Steward to hear residents' feedback.
