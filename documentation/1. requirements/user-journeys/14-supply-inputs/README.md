# Supply inputs

**Goal:** Sets prices, answers the farmers' asks and fills their orders.

**Result:** Farmers can buy from him at a price they can see, and he stays in charge of his stock.

**Who:** The supplier

The setting is the farmers' agency, one example agency among the demo's eight.

A shop owner applies to sell to a farmers' group, posts prices in his own words, answers farmers' asks, revises a group order and records payment, while keeping his own stock count private.

Mockups: [all steps in order](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/index.html).

## Steps

### 1. Applies and is approved

Apply as a supplier is a row on the Start tab. He sends his shop name, his area and what he sells. His application shows Waiting beside the Market manager with no time limit, and is also a row on the Ongoing tab. When the Market manager approves, his Inbox says he is approved as a supplier and his Start tab gains the supplier's rows: Offers, Asks, Group order, Orders, Your prices and Forms.

Mockup: [step-01-applies-and-is-approved.html](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/mockups/step-01-applies-and-is-approved.html)

### 2. Posts prices and keeps a private stock list

Offers lists what he sells in his own words, each with its price and bulk price: Urea 50kg at KES 3,800 a bag and KES 3,600 from 20 bags, DAP 50kg at KES 4,500 a bag. Add an offer takes the product as he writes it, the unit, the price, and the bulk minimum and bulk price if he has one. A row opens the same form to change it. A posted price answers by itself, so farmers do not need to ask him. Beside Offers sits his Stock list, the bags he has of each product, which only he sees. It is his own count, and he stays in charge of his stock. Before he posts anything the page is one card with Add an offer in it.

Mockup: [step-02-posts-prices.html](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/mockups/step-02-posts-prices.html)

### 3. Answers an ask

Asks lists the asks waiting for him, each with the farmer's name and what she wants. Opening one shows, for example, 2 bags of Urea 50kg needed by Friday, KES 7,600 at KES 3,800 a bag, with his own list beside it for him only: "Your list: 40 bags, already promised: 2 bags". Yes and No are his own buttons. Yes asks for a date (Friday, 6 Nov) and a note ("Collect at the shop"), and the farmer then sees his yes. If he does not answer, the ask stays waiting, with no timer.

Mockup: [step-03-answers-an-ask.html](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/mockups/step-03-answers-an-ask.html)

### 4. Sees a group order and asks the farmers to revise

Group order shows the open Urea order: the terms it started with, KES 3,600 a bag from 20 bags, and 16 of 20 bags so far from 8 farmers, started by Grace Adhiambo. He sees each farmer's name and bags and no contact details. Ask the farmers to revise opens a form for a new price a bag and the number of bags it starts from, for example KES 3,700 from 20. The page then shows the new terms above the original ones, and each farmer's own answer beside her bags: 3 yes, 2 no, 3 waiting. A farmer who says nothing shows Waiting.

Mockup: [step-04-sees-a-group-order.html](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/mockups/step-04-sees-a-group-order.html)

### 5. Marks an order paid

Orders lists his open yeses and orders under Open, each with the farmer, the bags, the date he gave and the total, and a Paid list for the ones done. Opening one shows the order as it was made, for example 2 bags of Urea 50kg at KES 3,800 a bag, KES 7,600 in all, with his date and note. Mark paid is his own button, and the order then shows Paid. Nothing here takes a payment; he records that it was paid.

Mockup: [step-05-marks-an-order-paid.html](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/14-supply-inputs/mockups/step-05-marks-an-order-paid.html)
