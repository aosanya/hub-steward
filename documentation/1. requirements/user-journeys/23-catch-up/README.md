# Catch up

**Goal:** A member sees what is new in her agency: the Inbox first, then her rooms.

**Result:** She knows what is waiting and which rooms have news.

Screens: [all four in order](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/23-catch-up/index.html).

## Steps

1. **She opens the Inbox.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/23-catch-up/mockups/step-01-opens-the-inbox.html). The Inbox lists what reached her in the Siaya Farmers' Group, newest first. A dot marks each unread row, the Inbox tab carries the unread count, and Mark all read sits in the header while anything is unread. Read rows have no dot.
2. **She sees nothing new.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/23-catch-up/mockups/step-02-sees-nothing-new.html). In an agency where nothing has arrived, such as the Siaya Community Computer Lab, the Inbox shows one card that says so. There is no button, no badge and no Mark all read.
3. **She opens the Chat tab.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/23-catch-up/mockups/step-03-opens-the-chat-tab.html). The tab lists her rooms, each with its latest message, the time and an unread count. Asha Otieno's room has two unread.
4. **She sees a busy room.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/23-catch-up/mockups/step-04-sees-a-busy-room.html). A room with more than 99 unread messages shows 99+, and the count stops there.

## What the drawings invent

The names, messages and times are drawn for the page; they are not data from a real agency.
