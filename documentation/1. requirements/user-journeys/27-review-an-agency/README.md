# Review an agency

**Who:** the designer.

**Goal:** The designer presses one button and AI reviewers read the whole draft at once, one reviewer for each part, and she reads their findings one page per part.

**Result:** Every reviewer has read the draft, the findings are saved, and she has applied, dismissed or commented on each one. A later review reads her comments and says what became of every earlier finding.

Review is a page of its own in the Agency menu, above Publish, with one row for each part under it: Profile, Goals, Objectives (only in the farmers' agency), Roles, Workflows, Risks and Tests. Profile reads the agency's name and description and checks that the description says who it serves. Review never blocks Publish, and it is not the Review stage of [Design an agency](../26-design-an-agency/README.md).

Screens: [all seven in order](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/index.html).

## Steps

1. **She opens Review.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-01-opens-review.html). With no review yet, one card says so and carries Run review. Otherwise the latest review shows as a card for each part, with its open count or Failed, and the parts are listed under Review in the menu.
2. **She runs the review.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-02-runs-the-review.html). One progress card says "Reading 3 of 7 parts" until every reviewer has finished, and then the part cards appear. She may leave the page and come back to the same run.
3. **She reads a part.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-03-reads-a-part.html). She clicks a card, or a part under Review in the menu. That part's page opens with a two-sentence summary and one row per finding.
4. **She answers a finding.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-04-answers-a-finding.html). Apply changes the draft and marks the finding Applied; a finding that names no change shows Enhance instead, which asks the chat to resolve it. Dismiss marks it Dismissed, and a dismissed finding offers Reopen. Details opens a tab, and Ask the chat about this opens a question box whose text goes to the chat. The state is saved.
5. **She comments on a finding.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-05-comments-on-a-finding.html). Add a note on the finding row opens a note box, and Save note keeps her note under the finding. The next review reads it.
6. **She runs the review again.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-06-runs-the-review-again.html). The new review reads the previous one and her comments, then reads the whole draft again. Each earlier finding is answered with a fate: fixed, still open or withdrawn. A fixed or withdrawn finding leaves the part's page, a still open one stays without a mark, and a finding found for the first time in this review shows New. A dismissed finding stays dismissed unless her comment no longer explains it.
7. **She finds a part that failed.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/27-review-an-agency/mockups/step-07-finds-a-part-that-failed.html). The part's card says Failed and its page gives the reason. The other parts keep their findings.
