# Answer a form

**Who:** a member.

**Goal:** A member answers a form her group sent, one question at a time, and sees where it went.

**Result:** The answers are sent once and the form moves to Answered.

Screens: [all six in order](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/index.html).

## Steps

1. **She sees the forms waiting.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-01-sees-the-forms-waiting.html). Forms opens on Waiting. It lists the open forms from every group she is in, closing soonest first, under Closes this week and Later. Each row shows who sent the form, how many questions it has and when it closes. A form with answers already kept on the phone is marked Draft.
2. **She answers one question at a time.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-02-answers-one-question-at-a-time.html). Each answer is kept on the phone as she gives it. Next opens the next question; on the last question Next opens Review.
3. **She reviews and sends.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-03-reviews-and-sends.html). Review lists every question with her answer and an Edit beside each. Nothing is sent before she presses Submit.
4. **She sees where it went.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-04-sees-the-thread-it-opens.html). After she sends, Form sent says her answers were sent, with Back to forms. For a standing form the screen is titled with the form and shows her answers one per line under Your thread · with the agent. The app does not open the chat thread itself.
5. **She finds what she answered.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-05-finds-what-she-answered.html). Forms, Answered lists the open forms she has answered, newest first, each with the date of her last send. A quiet button at the foot, Every form you answered, opens every form she answered, each marked Open or Closed.
6. **She has nothing waiting.** [Mockup](../../../2.%20design/mockups/journeys/24-answer-a-form/mockups/step-06-has-nothing-waiting.html). When no form is waiting, Waiting shows zero and one card, Nothing is waiting for you. The card carries its own button, See what you answered, which switches to Answered.

## What the drawings invent

The forms, senders, counts, dates and answers (Market gate, Siaya hospital) are examples drawn for the page; they are not forms of the demo agencies.

## Not yet built

- Answering a question with a photo or a file: the screen opens a file picker, but only the file's name is kept and sent. The file itself is not uploaded.
- A list of standing forms to start from: the Forms screen can show one, but the app's live client returns none.
- The thread a send opens in chat: step 4 shows a confirmation, or for a standing form her answers on a card, and no screen takes her into the thread.
